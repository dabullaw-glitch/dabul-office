# Design notes, round 2 (10.10.2026). Yakir: round 1 "boring, brown and cold, generic". Needs to sell.

## Precheck ("בדיקת דירה לפני קנייה")
Idea: "what the ad doesn't tell you". The memorable thing is an interactive reveal in the hero:
a glossy apartment ad on top, and you drag a handle to see the same apartment as the documents see it
(blueprint plan with red stamps and yellow highlights: parking not attached, balcony closed without a permit,
mortgage and caveat on the registry extract). One auto-sweep on load, then it's the visitor's.
- Color: blueprint ink #0E2A57, blueprint line #7FA6F0, paper #F6F8FC, stamp red #D33A2C, highlighter #FFE14D, ok green #17915A.
  No brown, no cream.
- Type: Karantina (condensed poster Hebrew) for the big moments: headline, price, stamps. IBM Plex Sans Hebrew for everything else.
- Layout: dark blueprint hero split (copy + reveal), then a "findings wall" of document fragments (each one a real,
  typical finding, with a stamp), then the actual answer they get (mini report), then a 3-point timeline, price with
  included/not included, the order form inside a blueprint frame, questions.
- Changed from my first instinct: dropped the big-number tiles and identical cards; the findings are torn document
  fragments with different stamps, not a card grid.

## Course ("קונים דירה ראשונה, בלי הפתעות")
Idea: the route. 10 lessons are 10 stations on one line, from "מצאתי דירה" to "המפתח בידיים". The line draws itself once
in the hero; on the page the lesson list *is* the route map (vertical on phones).
- Color: night indigo #1C1D4F, route mint #2FD39A, sun #FFC83D, white #FFFFFF, mist #EEF0FF, text #23244A.
- Type: Secular One (rounded, friendly) for headings and station names; Rubik for body.
- Layout: indigo hero with the route, "4 mistakes this course is built to prevent", the route of lessons,
  what's inside, who teaches, price ticket, order form, questions. Sticky buy bar on phones.
