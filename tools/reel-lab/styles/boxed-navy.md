# Style "boxed-navy" (Yakir, 12.10.2026)

Yakir pointed at his own short "פירוק שיתוף בירושה: כשאחד מסרב למכור" (published on YouTube and the networks, edited with the
reel editor v2, commit 0a7f9b9) and asked to use this look too, mixed with the others ("כל פעם אחד מהם").
Rotation from now on: our v3 look (the demo he approved, with his notes), "cinematic-gold", and this one.

## The look
- Name card top right: dark navy glass box with a thin gold frame, gold "ד" logo square, "עו״ד יקיר דבול" white bold,
  "משרד עורכי דין · נתניה" small grey under it.
- Topic pill top left: gold rounded pill with the field in dark text ("ירושה", "מקרקעין", "שכירות" ...).
- Hook box: wide navy box with a gold bottom border across the chest/neck line (below the chin, never on the face),
  two lines: the question in white, the second line in gold ("אח אחד מסרב? / תקועים לנצח?").
- Captions: navy rounded boxes with a gold underline, white bold text, one short phrase, low on the frame.
- Colours: navy #13254A to #1A2C55, gold #E4B84A, white. Real background of the take kept (no blur).
