## ref2-documentary.jpg (Yakir, 14.10.2026: "תשקיע ותעשה גם לי איכות כזאת")
The quality bar for every reel from now on. What makes it work:
- **Split screen**: the speaker sits in the lower half of the frame; the upper half is a dark stage for motion graphics. Graphics never cover the face.
- **A timeline bar** at the very top (years with a moving gold marker) that advances as the story moves in time. For us: the stages of a deal (חיפוש, בדיקות, משא ומתן, חוזה, אחרי החתימה, תשלומים, מסירה, רישום), with the marker on the current stage.
- **Dark, cinematic B-roll** between talking parts (night street, city lights), with a thin gold line "drawing" over it.
- **Big numbers in gold** that count up (for us: days, percent, number of steps, never prices or promised results).
- **Short key phrase per sentence** as a large red or gold caption placed between the graphics and the face, one line, 3 to 5 words.
- **UI and document close-ups** (screens, a form, a nesach) shown full width in the upper half with a slow zoom to the important line. Every personal detail blurred.
- **Logo/icon animations** made of particles that gather into a shape. For us: the office mark or a simple icon (key, house, document).
- Constant motion: something moves every 1 to 2 seconds, but cuts follow the speech.

## ref1-ad-style.jpg (Yakir, 13.10.2026, for the paid insolvency ad, script ad01)
- Direct to camera, full frame, one or two big words on screen per sentence.
- One keyword per sentence in color (for example "יש דרך" in light green).
- One 2-second atmospheric shot (letters, a bill) with no identifiable real person.
- No music. Meta rule: never imply that the viewer has debts. Bar rules: no promises, no "חינם", no prices.
